The journey so far

Before the reels and rooftop bars, there was an actual bar. I cut my teeth managing bars across the French Riviera and the Alps. In Saint-Tropez, Courchevel, then Deauville in Normandy, before heading behind the bar on a five-star cruise ship. Then I worked in Melbourne, Australia. Back in London, hospitality management at Aubaine Mayfair turned into a move behind the camera. I spent two years leading social media and events for Aubaine. I am currently the Social Media & Marketing Manager at AMANO Covent Garden. My journey so far spans fourteen years, four countries, with one throughline — understanding what makes people show up, stay a while, and come back.
